1 · Set up the booth

With a green screen, use steady lighting and avoid green clothes. The AI option loads an open-source person-segmentation model; it may not work on every phone.

2 · Capture the couple

Photograph the bride and groom with space on their right. Use your green screen, or try the free local AI option.

Camera locked in place. After capture, EverAfter cuts out the couple and creates the translucent live guide.

3 · Guest joins the couple

Stand on the RIGHT of the translucent couple. The booth digitally adapts height and spacing after capture.

✓ Adaptive digital height framing is on by default. It fits the guest into the final portrait without moving the camera.

Automatic digital framing is not mechanical camera movement. Keep the entire head and body in the original wide DSLR shot and enable autofocus on the camera.

4 · Your photograph

Operator tip

A fixed, wide, vertical frame + enough depth of field lets the software crop around tall and short people. If a subject is physically cut off or out of focus in the source, software cannot restore those missing details.
