1 · Set up the booth

The couple photo stays intact. Free AI removes the guest background, then places the guest INSIDE that same photograph. First use downloads a free model.

With a fixed camera, photograph the empty scene first. Do not move the camera, furniture, or lights between photos. Best on a plain backdrop; imperfect edges are possible.

DSLR folder watch works in Chrome / Edge on a computer, if your tether software saves JPEGs locally. Safari: use the DSLR upload below.

Key to realism: Photograph the couple toward the LEFT side with open space on the RIGHT, from the same camera distance and lighting as the guest. No background photo is substituted.

2 · Photograph the couple — leave room for guest

Capture the REAL wedding background. Place the couple on the left, leaving free space at the right for the guest. No AI is needed for this photo.

The original couple image remains the background for the finished photograph. It is not cut out.

3 · Guest joins the couple

Photograph the guest from the same distance and lighting. Free AI removes only the guest background, placing them inside the couple picture.

The faded couple image is only a live alignment guide; it is NEVER saved as a transparent ghost.

Keep heads and bodies within the full source picture. Digital cropping cannot restore missing details or correct a physically out-of-focus image.

4 · Together in one photograph

One unified 4×6 photograph: the original couple scene with the guest physically placed into it. Not a collage.

OPTIONAL OPERATOR FINISHING — adjust if the automatic fit looks unnatural:

Print uses the browser print dialog. The AI must successfully isolate the guest for a final photo; if not, choose the fixed-background or green-screen method. DSLR shutter control, direct printer drivers and SMS are not connected.

Operator tip

One photo, one scene: capture the couple with room for a guest. Use a wide, fixed camera with matching perspective. Auto-fitting reduces manual framing adjustments but cannot fix physically missed focus or clipped heads.
